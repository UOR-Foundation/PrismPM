# Private publication admission

OC-09 is a conditional source reducer, not a publisher or authentication service.
Public Pages deployment remains refused. OCI `candidate`/`accepted` trust states
do not substitute for the stages below.

## Bound context

The proved producer declaration selects a stage, complete pre-publication and
deployment-only obligation partition, exact target, policy revision, authority
identities and clock domain. It cannot embed its own future release digest.
Independent source-free replay supplies the concrete producer revision, release,
model, build, service/control/dependency/tool closure and artifact-tree identities.
The generated codec projects both identity preimages. Declaration identity uses
UTF-8 `prismpm/publication-declaration/1`, one NUL byte and canonical declaration
CBOR. Context identity uses UTF-8 `prismpm/publication-context/1`, one NUL byte
and the canonical six-field context array, omitting only its own digest field.
The host hashes these exact generated bytes with SHA-256; it must not reconstruct
a second declaration serializer. The supplied identities remain conditional
here; actual source linkage, hashing and authentication are independent gates.

No authority, provider, administrator, assessment policy or waived obligation is
chosen by this reducer. Foundry's complete five-service core belongs in its
producer declaration. Other required stages are not implicitly accepted.

## Transitions

| Revision | State | Required new facts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Unready | Bound context |
| 1 | ProducerReady | Replayed release trust, every pre-publication obligation, exact readiness receipt |
| 2 | DeploymentAuthorized | Independent decision and protected-ref authority over this readiness, context and publisher revision |
| 3 | DeploymentAuthorized | Actual deployment identity and independent exact-byte observation |
| 4 | Accepted | Every declared deployment-only obligation |

The exact deployment-only obligation set remains visible in the declaration
throughout. It cannot include unfinished pre-publication work. At least one
obligation exists in each phase. Deployment identity and byte observation are
unconditionally required independently of the selectable assessment inventory.

Evidence binds the context, obligation, assurance kind, authority, scope and
receipt. Failed or unknown outcomes are rejected. Formal proof, oracle result,
human assessment and observation are distinct kinds. One cannot satisfy another.
Every transition rechecks all retained evidence at the supplied observation tick;
clock rollback, expired facts, stale revisions and repeated transitions fail
without changing state. Clock domain, origin/unit and authority are source-owned;
ticks are unsigned 32-bit observations, never ambient time or invented expiry.
Recorded readiness/authorization/observation ticks remain in state. Deployment
cannot predate authorization or its decision's validity; byte observation cannot
predate that deployment. Historical facts must also fit their recorded phase.
No automatic retry, authorization renewal, rollback or accepted-release promotion
occurs. Republish/rollback needs a new authenticated attempt and the same gates.

The private host must independently replay source/model/artifact closure,
cryptographic trust and decision/ref authority, provider deployment identity,
OC-08 byte observation and each actual assessment. Arbitrary serialized facts,
digests, successful schema parsing or reducer output do not establish those facts.
The host owns authenticated state/CAS and must not accept a caller-created state.

## Bounds and wire

Deterministic CBOR uses definite minimal arrays, unsigned 32-bit integers,
byte strings and UTF-8 text; no maps, tags, floats or trailing bytes. Digests are
32 bytes; source/publisher/deployment revisions are 20 bytes. There are at most
4096 sorted unique obligations/facts, 2048 UTF-8 bytes per identifier, and 64 MiB
per complete request/result. These are metadata frames, not embedded artifacts.
The owning suite measures the actual combined structural maximum; no padded
64 MiB metadata frame is advertised as an admitted product operation.
Source inventories use at most 64 chunks of 64 entries, with only the final
chunk permitted to be shorter. The wire and identity preimages remain flat
arrays with identical order and coverage; internal chunking is not a new wire
representation or permission to omit an obligation.

Request `[1, operation, ...]`: initialize `0`; transition `1`; inspect current
state `2`; declaration preimage `3`; context preimage `4`. Projection requests
take the complete corresponding typed value and return `[1,3,preimage-bytes]`.
State results are `[1,0,state]` or `[1,1,protocol-error]`;
wire failure `[1,2,cbor-error]`. Constructor tags and record order are defined
by the source declarations and the accompanying CDDL.
